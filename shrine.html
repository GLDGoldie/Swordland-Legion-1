SHRINE team

Your building is the Swordshrine, centre of the map. It opens at 15:00, scores 9,000 on capture and 1,800 a minute, and decides the match. Until 10:00 the whole team is at the Stables with the Stables team: joiners put a march in it, Dark Rad, President Hades and Leeroy5 burn the enemy cities next to it. Nobody leaves until Kronk Fil says the Stables is secure; after that the three help whichever home team asks. At 10:00 everyone pulls back to the Shrine.

Your team, your call. The goal is the building. The roles and priorities below are a starting point, not orders. If the lead sees a better way to hold it, change the plan. Nobody outside the team needs to be asked.

Formations. Into a building or a city: your garrison formation. Into a rally: your attack-joiner formation. Gathering: five troops. Discuss the details with your team.
